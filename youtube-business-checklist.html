Question 1
Question 01 · The person

I can picture the one person I want to talk with when I turn on the camera.

A channel feels personal when the viewer recognizes that the conversation is meant for someone like them—not a broad crowd.

Question 2
Question 02 · The person

I understand the change that person wants to make in work, business, or the next chapter of life.

People follow when your conversations keep returning to something they genuinely want to change, build, escape, or understand.

Question 3
Question 03 · Your experience

I know which experiences, mistakes, results, or observations give me something worth saying.

Your advantage after 50 is not perfect credentials. It is the judgment and point of view created by things you have actually lived through.

Question 4
Question 04 · Point of view

I can name the conventional belief my channel will challenge or replace.

Strong channels do more than share information. They help the right viewer see an old problem through a clearer and more useful lens.

Question 5
Question 05 · Point of view

I can name at least three recurring conversations I would be glad to keep having.

You do not need a tiny niche, but you do need a recognizable thread connecting your stories, opinions, questions, and experiments.

Question 6
Question 06 · Business path

I know the one small problem a free checklist, assessment, or tool could solve next.

A useful free next step moves the relationship beyond YouTube and gives the viewer a concrete reason to join your email list.

Question 7
Question 07 · Business path

I know what I will continue talking about after someone joins my email list.

Email should continue the relationship and worldview from the channel—not become an unrelated stream of tips, links, and promotions.

Question 8
Question 08 · Business path

I can describe one meaningful result someone might eventually pay me to help produce.

The channel becomes a business when trust connects to a real problem, a useful result, and an offer people can choose to buy.

Question 9
Question 09 · Readiness

I know whether I want to build this myself, build it with guidance, or have the full system built with me.

A clear build preference prevents months of half-finished work and determines the right next step, timeline, and level of support.
