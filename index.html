What the checklist diagnoses

The distinction that matters

Don’t become a YouTuber. Build a business using YouTube.

A channel may have

  • Videos and topics
  • Views and subscribers
  • A publishing schedule
  • Advice with nowhere to lead

A YouTube-led business also has

  • A specific person and desired change
  • A clear and useful point of view
  • An owned email relationship
  • A meaningful paid result to validate
